Sneak peek

Giga-Ball 1.3

The biggest update since the game came out: a second endless mode, a challenge that changes every day, and a field full of bricks that do things. Arriving alongside iOS 27, in a month or two.

New mode

Endless Mayhem

Endless mode, let off the leash. The field descends as it always did, but now the bricks fight back: they fall, wander, spin, explode, refill their neighbours, and open portals across the screen. New power-ups act on the paddle rather than the ball, and there are enough of them that no two runs go the same way.

22 new power-ups Portals Moving bricks Its own leaderboard
New every day

The Daily Challenge

One challenge a day, the same for everybody, generated from the date itself. It might be a classic level or an endless run, and it comes with twists: one life, no power-ups, a field you cannot see until you hit it. You get one scoring attempt, and the board resets at midnight UTC. Miss a day and you can still go back and play it for fun.

New shapes

Bricks that are not rectangles

Every brick in the game has been a rectangle since 2020, so every bounce has been one of four answers. Three new shapes change that: a dome that scatters an off-centre hit, a dish that gathers one back toward the middle, and a wedge that sends everything reaching its slope the same way. The wedge is the closest the field comes to a brick you can aim with.

And the rest of it

Smaller things, mostly asked for by the people who play it.

Ring timers

Power-up timers moved from a bar under each icon to a ring around it, so you can read what is running at a glance.

Run stats

Every endless run now ends with its own numbers: height, time, paddle hits, bricks destroyed, and which power-ups you kept missing.

Recents

Pause mid-run and the reference pages lead with what you have just seen, rather than making you hunt the catalogue for it.

Carry on

Close the app mid-run and it picks up where you left off, daily challenges included.
