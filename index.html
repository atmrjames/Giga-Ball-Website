Giga-Ball

Bricks. A ball. One very busy paddle.

A brick breaker built the way they used to be, then given somewhere to go. 110 handmade levels, an endless mode that keeps coming, and power-ups that are as likely to ruin your run as save it.

Not a video. It is playing itself, badly.

Two ways to play

Work through the packs, or see how long you last when the field stops ending.

Classic

Eleven packs of ten levels, each one drawn by hand rather than generated. Space invaders, food, faces, numbers - you will recognise most of them before you have cleared them.

Endless

The field descends and does not stop. One ball, one life, and a height to beat - yours, and everybody else's.

Power-ups

Twenty-eight of them, and not all of them are on your side. Lasers, giant balls and sticky paddles fall alongside the ones that shrink your paddle and take your points.

Unlocks

Clearing packs earns ball and paddle themes, alternate app icons and new backgrounds. Nothing is for sale - it is all just in there.

Game Center

Leaderboards for every pack and for endless mode, plus a long list of achievements, some of which are quite silly.

Everywhere at once

Progress, unlocks and statistics follow you between iPhone and iPad over iCloud. Stop on one, carry on with the other.

Free, and free of the usual nonsense.

No adverts. No in-app purchases. No sign-up, no tracking, and no analytics. Made by one person who wanted a brick breaker worth keeping on the home screen.

Support

Found a bug, or want to argue about a power-up? Email contact@giga-ball.app and you will hear back from the person who made it.
