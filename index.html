Classic Mode

Eleven packs of ten levels, each one drawn by hand. Space invaders, food, faces, numbers - you will recognise most of them before you have cleared them.

Endless Mode

The field descends and does not stop. One ball, one life, and a height to beat - yours, and everybody else's.

Power-ups

Twenty-eight of them, and not all are on your side. Lasers, giant balls and sticky paddles fall alongside the ones that shrink your paddle and take your points.

Unlocks

Ball and paddle themes, alternate app icons and new backgrounds. Nothing is for sale - play to unlock the content.

Game Center

Leaderboards for every pack and for endless mode, plus a long list of achievements, some of which are quite silly.

Everywhere at once

Progress, unlocks and statistics follow you between iPhone and iPad over iCloud. Stop on one, carry on with the other.
